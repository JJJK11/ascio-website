How the engagement runs

Five steps, demand to reusable answer

The same sequence in both delivery modes. Self guided, your team runs it with ascio reviewing at fixed points; guided, ascio runs it with you. Every stage produces an artifact you keep.

  1. Read the demand

    The questionnaire, contract clause, or renewal form, turned into a precise list of what is actually being asked.

  2. Map what exists

    Policies, controls, and records you already have, matched against the ask. Most companies hold more than they think, in worse order than they think.

  3. Fill the gaps

    The missing policies and controls, sequenced by what blocks revenue first.

  4. Assemble the response library

    Answers written once, mapped to the questions buyers actually ask, with the evidence behind each.

  5. Answer and reuse

    The live request answered, and the library kept so the next one is a lookup, not a project.

Questions

Do we need a certification to satisfy a customer questionnaire?

Usually not. Most questionnaires ask about controls and evidence, not certificates. If a customer does require certification, that is the ISO Readiness package.

The Rescue fee is credited against a Buyer Assurance Pack bought within 60 days. The Rescue clears the blocking questionnaire; the Pack makes sure the next one is not an emergency.

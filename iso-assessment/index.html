Engagement terms

  1. The assessment is provided free of charge and without obligation. It is an evaluation to help you understand your readiness; it is not a certification, audit, or guarantee of any outcome.
  2. Each application covers one standard (ISO/IEC 27001, ISO/IEC 42001, or CyberSecure Canada). You may apply separately for additional standards.
  3. The engagement consists of three meetings totalling two hours: a 45 minute discovery meeting, a 45 minute interview meeting, and a 30 minute findings walkthrough, plus a short survey completed between meetings.
  4. On receipt of your application, the ascio intake team will confirm receipt by email and arrange dates for your meetings.
  5. You receive a written readiness report summarizing your current state, gaps, and prioritized next steps, reviewed with you in the final walkthrough.
  6. Applications must be submitted by June 15, 2026. Information you share is used only to deliver this assessment and is handled in line with our privacy policy.
